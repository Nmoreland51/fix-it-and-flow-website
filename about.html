About FixIt & Flow

A side hustle built around useful skills, trust, and real-life problem solving.

I started FixIt & Flow because I wanted a job or side hustle that connected to me personally. I wanted to build something practical that helps people with problems I understand: phone issues, basic tech troubleshooting, and messy information that needs to be organized into clean spreadsheets.

My Story

FixIt & Flow began because I wanted to create a business that felt useful and realistic for me. I did not want a random idea that sounded good only on paper. I wanted something connected to everyday problems people actually have.

Phones are a big part of daily life, so when a screen cracks, a charger stops working, or the speaker has issues, people need clear and affordable help. At the same time, many small businesses, sellers, freelancers, and everyday people have information that is messy, unorganized, or hard to use.

This business brings those two needs together. The "FixIt" side focuses on phone repairs and basic troubleshooting. The "Flow" side focuses on data entry, spreadsheet cleanup, inventory lists, customer lists, and simple templates that make work feel more organized.

The goal is to make repairs and spreadsheets feel less stressful, more understandable, and easier to afford.

What Makes Me Different

Customers should choose FixIt & Flow because the service is simple, local, and beginner-friendly.

  • What do you do that competitors don't? I offer two helpful services in one place: phone repair help and spreadsheet organization. Customers can get a device issue fixed and messy information cleaned up without dealing with a big repair shop, a confusing tech service, or an expensive agency.
  • What special training or experience do you have? I have hands-on experience with basic phone troubleshooting, problem solving, data entry, and spreadsheet organization. I know how to break a problem down, figure out what needs to be fixed, and turn messy information into something clear and useful.
  • What do your customers say they love about working with you? Customers appreciate that I explain things in a simple way, give clear quotes before work starts, and keep the process easy to understand. I want people to feel comfortable asking questions, even if they are not tech experts.
  • What extra mile do you go? I take time to listen, double-check the work, and make sure the customer understands what was fixed or organized. For spreadsheet projects, I try to leave the file neat enough that the customer can keep using it after the job is done.
